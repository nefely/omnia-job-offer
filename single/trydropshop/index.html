About Dropshop

Dropshop is an innovative take on ecommerce that combines rewarded brand discovery and online discount shopping.
Our web-based platform gives consumers deep discounts on unique products, which is made possible by leveraging rewarded brand discovery. Essentially, products are priced at or near $0. Consumers are asked to complete two tasks, which fund the cost and the shipping of the product.
Based in New York City, we are aiming to partner with over 10,000 product manufacturers over the next 12 months and will cover categories ranging from outdoor sports to pet supplies and home décor.
The combination of discount pricing and consumer-initiated brand discovery is a powerful tool for millions of people to save money while providing product manufacturers and service providers with a unique channel to engage new customers.
