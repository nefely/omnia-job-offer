To earn the $750 Reviewer Bonus you must:

  • 1. Successfully complete 20 Deals and have the initial $750 reward fulfilled as part of this limited time promotion. Once completed, you will receive an email with specific instructions on how to submit your Product Review.

    • · Provide a copy of your itemized product order confirmation. (Order date must be later than your original reward received date)
    • · Your product order needs to match with the name and email address used for your original reward.
    • · From your order, pick what you want to review, and include the item(s) name and SKU number(s).
    • · Include your own pictures of the item(s) you’re reviewing. (You’ll need to take the picture, stock photos from their website don’t qualify!)
    • · Your Product Review must be a minimum of 100 words, describing what you liked and/or didn't like about the product(s).

Once we receive your Product Review and all qualifications are met, our team will fulfill your bonus $750 payment!
