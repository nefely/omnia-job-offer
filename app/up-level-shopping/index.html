About the app

UpLevelShopping is the ultimate shopping app that rewards you for your daily activities. With a user-friendly interface and a host of innovative features, it is the perfect tool to help you save money and get the best deals on your favorite products. more

To earn even more points, you can activate special codes that are available on the app. These codes are unique to each user and can be found in the activation conditions. Simply read the conditions, copy the code, and redeem it for extra points.
