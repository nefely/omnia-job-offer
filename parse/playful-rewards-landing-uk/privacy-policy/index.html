Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Playful Rewards ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share when visiting our website or participating in our mobile app testing and rewards programme.

1. Information We Collect

  • Usage & Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device identifiers, IP address, referring URLs, and interaction timestamps used to verify offer eligibility and prevent fraud.
  • Account & Payout Information: When you register in the Playful Rewards app, you may provide an email address and payment details (such as PayPal or gift card delivery email) solely to process rewards and cashouts.
  • Voluntary Submissions: Comments or support inquiries you choose to submit directly to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match you with compatible mobile games, apps, and promotional offers for your device and region.
  • To track offer completions, validate in-app milestones, and credit bonuses (including install bonuses and upgrade reimbursements).
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraudulent activity, VPN abuse, or multiple-account violations.
  • To respond to customer support requests and improve user experience.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies and browser storage (such as sessionStorage) to maintain session state, preserve offer reservation timers, and attribute partner referrals accurately. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some reward tracking features may not function properly.

4. Third-Party Partners & Advertisers

When you click through to download Playful Rewards or partner apps via the Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or verified offer partners, those third parties operate under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal contact information to unauthorized third parties.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls to safeguard your data. Information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil reward obligations, comply with legal requirements, and resolve disputes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, as well as the right to opt out of non-essential tracking.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended strictly for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us at support@playfulrewards.com.
