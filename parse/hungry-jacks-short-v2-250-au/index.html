    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the Rewards Club?

    Rewards Club is an independent rewards program that gives members the opportunity to earn gift cards and vouchers by completing surveys and deals. We are not affiliated with any retail brand.

    How do I qualify for the $250 reward?

    To receive the $250 reward, you need to complete the required number of deals across all levels as outlined in the Program Requirements. The reward is issued after verification of completed deals.

    What are "Deals"?

    Deals include free trials, mobile apps, subscription services, and other offers from our partners. Some deals are free, while others may require a purchase.

    Is there a purchase required to participate?

    No purchase is necessary to join the program. However, some deals within the program may require a purchase to complete.

    How will I receive my reward?

    Rewards are delivered digitally to the email address you provide during registration.

    How long does it take to receive my reward?

    After your deals are verified, delivery typically takes 3–7 business days.

    Who can participate?

    The program is open to US and AU residents who are 18 years of age or older.

    How do I contact support?

    You can reach our support team at reward@rewards.tremendous.com or through the Member Support page.
