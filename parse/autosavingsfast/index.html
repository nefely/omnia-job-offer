Smart Coverage: Extra Protection for Life's Uncertainties

When life throws unexpected situations your way, Smart Coverage can provide coverage where other insurance policies don't. Whether you're looking to protect your savings and assets, or simply want more robust insurance, Smart Coverage steps in when other policies reach their limits.

Key Takeaways

  • Smart Coverage offers additional liability coverage that extends beyond the limits of your auto, home, or boat insurance, covering incidents like lawsuits, property damage, and injuries.
  • With generally low costs relative to its high coverage limits, Smart Coverage provides a practical way to protect your savings and assets from unexpected, high-cost incidents.
  • Whether you have valuable assets to protect or want more robust coverage, Smart Coverage offers extra financial security and peace of mind.

How Smart Coverage Works

Smart Coverage, or Smart Coverage liability insurance, provides additional coverage that extends beyond the limits of a standard car or home insurance policy. If you're involved in a serious accident or face a lawsuit that exceeds your current coverage, an Smart Coverage policy can cover the extra costs, including legal fees, medical expenses, and damages.

Smart Coverage is especially valuable when you're facing high-cost incidents that put your personal finances at risk. It protects against larger liabilities and offers more coverage than your average policy. Essentially, it's designed to provide a safety net for liability-related situations that could otherwise be financially draining.

What Does Smart Coverage Cover?

Smart Coverage can provide:

  • Extended coverage where your home, car, and boat insurance policies may leave off.
  • Coverage for claims that aren't included in a typical insurance policy, like false arrest, libel, slander, and rental unit claims.
  • Coverage for injuries, property damage, lawsuits, and personal liability situations.

Smart Coverage Examples

Let's say you're involved in a car accident where multiple people are injured, or extensive property damage occurred. If you're found responsible, the costs can quickly add up and potentially exceed the liability limits of your car insurance policy. In this case, Smart Coverage would cover what your auto insurance doesn't, protecting you from costly out-of-pocket expenses.

Alternatively, if your dog bites someone and causes an injury, you could be held responsible. Even if your homeowner's insurance covers dog bites, it might not be enough if the claim is high. If you're responsible for the victim's injuries and their bill is higher than your coverage limits, Smart Coverage can cover any additional costs.

The Benefits of Smart Coverage

Smart Coverage can protect your finances against expenses from accidents and lawsuits. Here are the benefits of Smart Coverage:

Enhanced coverage

Liability claims can be costly, potentially draining your savings, home, or other valuable assets. Smart Coverage protects your finances by covering claims that traditional liability policies typically exclude.

Affordable high coverage limits

Smart Coverage policies are generally inexpensive relative to the amount of coverage they offer.

Household coverage

Smart Coverage covers you and other members of your household, including spouses or dependents.

Is Smart Coverage Right for You?

Smart Coverage is ideal for those with assets to protect or who may be at higher risk for lawsuits due to their lifestyle or job. It's a smart choice if you're seeking additional peace of mind and protection beyond your existing coverage. For many, the added peace of mind makes Smart Coverage a valuable addition to their current insurance.

In a world full of surprises, Smart Coverage provides valuable security. Whether you're protecting your family or finances, an Smart Coverage policy can give you confidence in your coverage.
