RewardClub Digest

A short weekly digest covering publicly-announced reward programs, cashback offers, and consumer savings tips — plus heads-ups when a "free reward" claim online looks suspicious. No hype, no spam, unsubscribe anytime.

What you'll get in each weekly edition:
  • Reward program roundup. A summary of publicly-announced loyalty rewards, cashback programs, and limited-time offers worth knowing about that week.
  • Savings tips. Practical, no-nonsense ways to get more value from reward programs, store memberships, and everyday purchases.
  • Scam & fake offer alerts. Quick notes when we spot a suspicious "free gift card" or survey-style promotion circulating online, so you know what to avoid.
