Consent

By using this website, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree, please do not use this website.

Information we collect

Information you give us

This website does not contain any sign-up form, and we do not ask you to enter your name, email address, postal address, date of birth, payment details or any other personal information in order to view this page.

If you choose to contact us by email, we will receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you send. We use that information only to respond to you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server log data when you visit. This may include your internet protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, Internet Service Provider (ISP), device and operating system, date and time stamp, referring and exit pages, and the pages you viewed. This information is used for analysing trends, administering the site, and diagnosing technical problems. It is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and our advertising partners use cookies, pixels, SDK-free web beacons and similar technologies to understand how visitors reach and use this page, and to measure the performance of our advertising. Specifically, this page uses:

  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — measures whether visitors who arrive from a paid social campaign go on to click through to the offer, and helps us understand which campaigns work. It may set cookies and share event data with Meta.
  • AppsFlyer — the measurement platform used for the official testerup tracking link. When you click the button on this page, AppsFlyer records the click so that testerup can attribute your visit to us.

These third parties automatically receive your IP address when these technologies load. We have no access to or control over the cookies that our advertising partners set. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; details are available on your browser’s own website. Disabling cookies will not prevent you from reading this page.

How we use your information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure this website;
  • Understand how visitors use the page and improve its content and layout;
  • Measure and optimise the performance of our advertising campaigns;
  • Attribute clicks to the offer so that our partner can pay us commission;
  • Respond to you if you contact us;
  • Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and technical issues;
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails from this website.

Legal bases for processing (UK and EEA visitors)

Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — for server logs, site security and basic, aggregated traffic analysis;
  • Consent — for advertising and measurement cookies, where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time through your browser or device settings;

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers, who process data on our behalf;

This Privacy Policy does not apply to other websites, advertisers or platforms. Once you click through to testerup, or to any other third-party site, their privacy policy governs. Server log data is retained for as long as our hosting provider retains it, typically no longer than 12 months. Email correspondence is kept only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry. Data held by our advertising partners is retained according to their own policies.

Your privacy rights

You are entitled to the following rights:

  • The right to access – to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – to ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate, or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • The right to erasure – to ask us to erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing – to ask us to restrict processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to object to processing – to object to our processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to data portability – to ask us to transfer data we have collected to another organisation, or to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
